WHO WE ARE

Engineering India CCOEW

Engineering India is a student-driven platform that brings together leadership, creativity, technical learning and social responsibility.

Through events, initiatives, workshops and community activities, Engineering India provides students with opportunities to learn beyond the classroom and contribute meaningfully to society.

The magazine reflects a journey filled with experiences, collaboration, leadership and memories created throughout the year.
